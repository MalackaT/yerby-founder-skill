## What would flip a no

- A free tasting can or a single can in a shop near me, so I know I like the taste before committing to a 12-pack.
- Tasting it after a long ride or run and finding it genuinely refreshing rather than grassy; then I'd consider a pack.
- Trying a free can at a launch event or at my bistro, and actually liking it. Seeing it on Vilgain or GymBeam would help too.
- Seeing it in the fridge at Albert, Billa or the gym or bike shop I already go to, so I can buy one single can and taste it first.
- A single can or a 4-pack in a normal shop on offer for around 35 CZK a can, so I could try it without buying twelve.
- Seeing it in the fridge at the petrol station or Albert I already stop at, around 40 to 45 CZK, and trying a free can that actually tastes good and kicks like a Monster.
- Seeing it in a fridge at a petrol station, Albert or Billa, or added to my next GymBeam or Vilgain order as a single can or small pack, at around 45 CZK.
- Trying a free cold can at a launch event or a partner café and actually feeling the kick, or seeing it in the fridge at Albert or a petrol station.
- Trying a free can at a launch event or café on a late study night and finding it keeps me going as well as my usual drink without the crash.
- Being able to buy a single cold can at Albert or a petrol station for around Red Bull money, or trying a free one first and it actually kicking in.
- Trying a free can at a launch event, or being able to buy a 4-pack or single cans on Rohlík's bio section for around 35-40 CZK a can.
- Seeing it cold in the fridge at petrol stations I stop at, or at GymBeam/Vilgain next to my pre-workout, so I can try one can for around 40 CZK.

Buyers say they would buy **1.3 times** in the first month on average.
