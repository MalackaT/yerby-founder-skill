# yerby

- What it is: A 100% natural, clean-label, cold-brewed, carbonated yerba mate drink in a 330 ml slim aluminium can; first SKU Lemon & Grapefruit.
- Who it is for: Mainstream energy-drink consumers in Czechia who want a healthier, cleaner, natural energy alternative without synthetic ingredients.
- What it sells, at what price: 330 ml cans at a target retail price of ~55 CZK (open to testing 55–79 CZK by channel); production cost ~24 CZK per can (incl. VAT, ~19.83 CZK ex-VAT).
- Where and how: Czechia. Pre-launch awareness via an email-capture funnel and founder-led Instagram/LinkedIn content. Planned channels: D2C online, direct B2B to cafés and bistros, wholesale distributors, later retail chains.
- Budget and constraints: yerby s.r.o., 50/50 co-founders, currently not a VAT payer. First commercial run of up to 3,000 cans with a production partner. Recipe approved, can design final, EAN-13 (GS1 GCP-9) ready. Cash budget not stated.
