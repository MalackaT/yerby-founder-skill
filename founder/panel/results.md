## What would flip a no

- Seeing it in the fridge at a petrol station or Albert so I could try one can on the way, ideally after a free sample proves it isn't bitter.
- Seeing it on Rohlík or GymBeam at around 35-40 CZK, or a multipack deal that brings the per-can price down to that.
- A free sample can after the gym or in a GymBeam order that proves it doesn't taste like grass, plus a multipack price closer to 35 a can.
- Seeing it on Rohlík or in the fridge at a café I actually go to, so I can grab one can on impulse.
- If it was in the fridge at Albert or Tesco for around 40-45 CZK, I'd try it on a yoga day instead of a Monster.
- Seeing it in the fridge at the petrol stations I stop at (Shell, OMV, Orlen) next to Red Bull, at about the same price.
- Seeing it in a supermarket multipack on offer at around 30 crowns a can, so I could try it without losing money.
- A stronger version with around 150 mg of caffeine, sold in multipacks on Vilgain or GymBeam at a lower price per can.
- A free sample or a multipack on Vilgain or GymBeam at around 35 CZK a can, so I could try it before a long ride without paying café prices.
- Seeing it on Rohlik in a multipack on offer at around 30 CZK a can.
- Seeing it in the fridge at Albert or Tesco at around 40 CZK, or a free sample that proves it doesn't taste like grass.
- A free can or a tasting at the café I eat at, so I could see whether it gets me through a shift without the crash. If it did, I'd swap it in for the afternoon can.

Buyers say they would buy **1.8 times** in the first month on average.
