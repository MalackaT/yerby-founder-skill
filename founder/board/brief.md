# Board brief: yerby

## The idea
- **Company:** yerby s.r.o., based in Czechia. Two co-founders, 50/50 split. Currently NOT registered as a VAT payer.
- **Product:** A 100% natural, "clean label", cold-brewed, carbonated yerba mate beverage in a 330 ml slim aluminium can. First commercial SKU: Lemon & Grapefruit.
- **Ingredients/process:** Only water, 12-hour cold-brewed yerba mate, and natural lemon and grapefruit concentrates. Filtered, carbonated, pasteurised. No synthetic additives, artificial flavours or chemical preservatives.
- **Target customer:** Mainstream energy-drink consumers in Czechia who want a healthier, cleaner, natural energy alternative without synthetic ingredients.
- **Brand:** Minimalist, modern, uncluttered: matte cans, English-language labels, GS1 GCP-9 EAN-13 barcode, ready for retail. Social voice: bold, confident, playfully provocative, contrasting natural yerba mate directly against conventional energy drinks and against matcha.

## Stage
Pre-launch. Final recipe approved; can design and layout final. Preparing the first commercial production run of up to 3,000 cans with a contract production partner.

## Numbers the founders gave (estimates)
- Production cost: ~24 CZK per can including VAT (they cannot reclaim VAT as non-payers); ~19.83 CZK ex-VAT.
- Target retail price: ~55 CZK per can; open to testing 55–79 CZK depending on channel and consumer feedback.
- First batch: up to 3,000 units.
- Not given: cash budget, marketing budget, wholesale/B2B price, distributor margins, caffeine content per can, shelf life, minimum order quantity of the production partner, founders' time commitment.

## Go-to-market
- Now: pre-launch awareness via an email-capture funnel and founder-led content on Instagram and LinkedIn.
- Planned channels: D2C online; direct B2B sales to cafés and bistros; wholesale distributors; long-term, retail chains.

## Notes for board members

Later steps answer them: competitor scan (/founder-competitors), simulated buyer panel (/founder-consumer), unit economics (/founder-cfo).
